# Ingestion architecture acceptance cases — Phase 0.0-E

These are deterministic **specification fixtures**, not executable tests. Unless stated otherwise, provider access is externally approved for the fixture, source snapshots are persisted before processing, identity is unambiguous, and finality is verified by the conservative status table. `PA`/`HR` below refer to game domain coverage; `PART` to participation. A failed candidate commit leaves prior published canonical facts unchanged and retains raw snapshot/issue. Any accepted fact or coverage change advances one dataset revision and invalidates relevant analytics; an identical no-op does neither.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 2 | Final zero-HR game, all PAs classified and boxscore HR zero | Publish verified zero HR; revision +1. | PA COMPLETE, HR COMPLETE. | None; empty HR list alone would be UNKNOWN. |

| 4 | Two same-date games, distinct gamePk and gameNumber 1/2 | Publish two Game UUIDs and ordered columns. | Each assessed independently. | No `(teams,date)` merge. |

For every fixture assert exact canonical identity counts, source references, current coverage and history, issue/quarantine state, revision increment/no-op, and affected analytics availability. Fixture 11 is backed by [E18](PROVIDER_EVIDENCE.md); fixtures 5–7 use sampled lifecycle/participation evidence. Other fixtures are synthetic architecture acceptance cases, not claims that every provider outcome has been observed.
